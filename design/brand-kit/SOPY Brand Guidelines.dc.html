01 — Brand

SOPY replaces the paper SOP binder with checklists that run on a phone, collect photo evidence and score every shift.

For

Owners, area managers and shift crews at restaurants and cafés.

Promise

Every store runs to the same standard, and you can prove it.

Character

Steady, exact, on the crew's side. A good kitchen manager, not an auditor.

02 — Logo

The Checkpoint mark

A procedure reduced to three lines. The last step ends in a dot, the moment a checkpoint is confirmed. The wordmark is set in Source Serif 4 Bold, keeping the serif voice the product already uses.

Primary · Forest on Paper or white
Reverse · Paper on Forest
One colour · Ink, for print and fax-grade forms
Mark and wordmark · Mark alone for icons and avatars. Wordmark alone only where the mark already appears.
Arabic lockup · سوبي in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold. Mark sits on the reading-start side.
Clear space · Half the mark's height on every side.
Minimum size
Lockup · 28px / 10mm
Mark · 16px / 5mm
Construction

Tile corner radius is 22% of the tile. Bars are 9.5% thick with 7.5% gaps. The dot is 17% of the tile. The wordmark cap height matches the height of the three bars.

Don't
Stretch or squash it
Rotate it
Place it on status colours
Add shadows or effects

06 — Voice

Say what happened, then what to do

People read SOPY mid-shift, often with wet hands. Keep it short, specific and calm.

Specific over vague
DOFridge 2 is at 7 °C. Safe range is 1–4 °C.
DON'TSomething looks off with a fridge.
Calm under pressure
DOCritical item failed. Your manager has been notified.
DON'TURGENT!! Compliance failure detected!
Credit the crew
DOOpening checklist done. 42 of 42 checkpoints.
DON'TSubmission 8841 processed successfully.
